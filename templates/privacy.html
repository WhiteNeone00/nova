Privacy Policy

1. Data We Collect

At Nova Serverside, we prioritize your privacy and collect only the information necessary to provide and secure our services. We collect:

  • Account Information: Email addresses and hashed passwords when you register.
  • Linked Accounts: Roblox or Discord account identifiers when you choose to link them to your Nova account.
  • Service Data: Server telemetry, execution logs, and analytics generated by your legitimate use of the dashboard.
  • Security Data: IP addresses and browser fingerprints to prevent abuse, track active sessions, and secure your account.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your access to the dashboard and API securely.
  • Provide performance analytics and execution tracking for your servers.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access to our infrastructure.
  • Communicate important updates regarding the service.

We do not use your data, scripts, or analytics for any malicious purposes, nor do we monitor them to gain an unfair advantage in third-party services.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may only disclose data if required by law, to enforce our Terms of Service, or to protect the safety and rights of Nova Serverside and its users.

4. Data Security

We employ industry-standard encryption, secure session management, and robust database architectures to protect your data. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect personal information, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your account information and telemetry data at any time via the dashboard. You may also contact our support team through Discord if you require assistance with data removal.

6. Cookies and Sessions

We use essential cookies and session tokens to keep you logged in and ensure your interactions with our API are secure. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.
